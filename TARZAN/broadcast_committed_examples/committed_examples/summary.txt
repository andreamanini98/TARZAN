clockCheck: In this example we check the property that a committed state cannot delay.
In this case the initial location is committed and has a transition to a committed location called checker,
since they are both commited the only possible value of the clock at the 
end of the transitions is 0. We formally test this by perform reachability on both Success and Failure 
locations.

operationsOrder: In this example we check that the once in a committed locations the only transitions are the one of the committed locations.
All the locations of the Sender2 are committed and the goal is to test that the Sender1 can never reach the Done location.
In this way we check that  the next transition from a committed location must involve an outgoing edge of at least one of the committed locations.

syncReceiver: In this example we check that the transitions are performed correctly even when the receiver reach a committed locations.
More in detail the example provided simulate a broadcast transition with the committed locations, by sending two signals (signal1, signal2) through an atomic sequence.


syncBroadcast: In this example we check that broadcast signals work correctly with committed locations.
In particular the sender performs the transition signal!! from Start to Done. We formally checked it performing 
the reachability analysis on the sender(Done) and Receivers(Updated).
syncSelfLoop: We check that syncBroadcast has the same expected outcome even when there is a self loop.
To test this we inserted a self loop in the Receiver1 from idle to idle.
